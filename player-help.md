## Protecting your stuff

Use Lockette to protect chests and doors: place a sign on a chest, or above a door (on iron doors place the sign to avoid powering them).

Protect blocks with Towny: create a town, then gain and maintain power (through survival) to keep block protection (except during wars).

## Jobs

Make money with Jobs (harvest, build, etc)! <br>
Type `/jobs browse`. <br>
After joining a job you can see your stats using `/jobs stats`.
